What to saySay direction + length together, then pause:
“up zero point five”, “down one point two”, “up right 0.3”, “north east two”, “down left five centimeter”.
Directions: up / down, up-right, up-left, down-right, down-left (or north, south, east, west combos). Hindi: ऊपर, नीचे, दाएं, बाएं.
Say direction and length in either order, or one after the other. “undo” removes the last piece, “stop” closes voice.

Hand signs👍 thumb up = ↑   👎 thumb down = ↓
☝ index finger pointing (other fingers closed): up-right = ↗, up-left = ↖, down-right = ↘, down-left = ↙ (straight up / down also work).
Hold the sign about 1 second, then say the length: “zero point five”, “one point two five”, “five centimeter”. Lower your hand before the next sign.
